Google Form to WhatsApp in Seconds

WhatsForm is a free Google Forms add-on that sends every form response directly to your WhatsApp. No coding, no extra apps. Customers fill your form and the data lands in your WhatsApp chat, ready for you to reply and build relationships.

How It Works

Set up in 4 simple steps

From install to receiving your first WhatsApp message in under a minute

1

Install the Add-on

Install WhatsForm from the Google Workspace Marketplace. It works right inside Google Forms.

2

Enter Your Number

Open the add-on inside any Google Form, enter your WhatsApp number and click create.

3

Share the Link

Share your new WhatsForm link with customers through any channel you already use.

4

Get Messages

Receive form responses directly on WhatsApp from the customer's own number.
